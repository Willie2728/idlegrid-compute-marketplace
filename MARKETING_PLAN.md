# IdleGrid go-to-market and MRR model

## Positioning

Do not market IdleGrid as unrestricted access to strangers' personal computers. Market it as **verified, isolated compute from real owner-operated machines**. Initial jobs: Blender rendering, Unreal builds, video export, Xcode builds, remote gaming, and GPU experiments. Personal files, cameras, microphones, owner credentials, and the owner's normal desktop remain inaccessible.

## Revenue model

IdleGrid retains 25% of hourly booking revenue and pays hosts 75%. The planning average is $7.60 per booked hour.

`Platform MRR = active renters × paid hours per renter × average hourly price × 25%`

| Scenario | Active monthly renters | Hours/renter | Average price | Monthly GMV | Host payouts | IdleGrid MRR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Early traction | 750 | 8 | $7.60 | $45,600 | $34,200 | **$11,400** |
| Base case | 2,000 | 10 | $7.60 | $152,000 | $114,000 | **$38,000** |
| Strong network effect | 5,000 | 12 | $8.00 | $480,000 | $360,000 | **$120,000** |

These are scenarios, not a prediction. The most defensible target is **$10,000–$15,000 MRR after 12 months**, conditional on reaching about 750 repeat renters and adequate machine supply. MRR excludes refunds, payment costs, insurance, support, fraud losses, taxes, and remote-access infrastructure.

## Capital required

### 90-day demand pilot: $150,000 total

- $30,000 paid media and creative testing
- $45,000 secure host agent, booking backend, and provider integration
- $20,000 security review and penetration testing
- $20,000 legal, marketplace terms, licensing review, and insurance setup
- $20,000 host onboarding, customer support, refunds, and incentives
- $15,000 contingency

If the existing team supplies engineering, a lean validation can run for **$40,000–$60,000**, with $15,000–$25,000 earmarked for ads and host incentives. A broader multi-city launch likely requires **$300,000–$500,000** after the pilot proves retention and unit economics.

## 90-day advertising allocation

| Channel | Budget | Audience and purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Reddit | $8,000 | Blender, Unreal Engine, video editing, PC gaming, homelab, Mac developer communities; direct-response landing pages by job |
| YouTube creator sponsorships | $7,000 | Small and mid-size render, game-dev, and creator channels; tutorials showing a real isolated task |
| Google Search | $6,000 | High-intent queries such as rent GPU PC, remote Mac for Xcode, cloud gaming PC, and hourly render workstation |
| TikTok / Instagram Reels | $4,000 | Short before-and-after exports, cost comparison, retargeting; discovery rather than primary conversion |
| Host referral credits | $3,000 | Acquire verified supply in the same geographic regions as demand |
| Creative and landing-page tests | $2,000 | Three task-specific ads, attribution, and conversion experiments |

Do not start with broad national Meta awareness campaigns. First prove one job and one audience. Reddit recommends objectives aligned to the outcome and suggests a daily budget around three times target CPA per ad group. Google and Reddit both support budget controls; auction prices still require testing against actual conversion data.

## Campaign sequence

1. Recruit 25–50 verified hosts before spending heavily on renters.
2. Launch three landing pages: Render Faster, Build on a Mac, and Game on a High-End PC.
3. Offer a first-session credit capped at $10; pay referral credit only after a completed, non-refunded session.
4. Retarget visitors who checked compatibility or pricing but did not book.
5. Scale only when 30-day renter repeat rate exceeds 25%, machine-session success exceeds 95%, refund rate stays below 5%, and payback is under three months.

## Sample ad copy

**Headline:** Borrow power. Not hardware.

**Body:** Your laptop should not decide what you can create. Book secure access to powerful gaming PCs, Macs, and creator workstations by the hour. Personal owner data stays isolated. Your session disappears when the work is done.

**CTA:** Find your machine

**Offer:** First session credit up to $10 after identity verification. Terms apply.
